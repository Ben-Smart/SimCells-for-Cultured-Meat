\hline
$C_{L,\text{in}}^\text{lac}$ & Influent concentration of lactic acid & \textbf{Experimental} --- set by media formulation or measured at inlet \\
\hline
$C_{L,\text{in}}^\text{NH}$ & Influent concentration of ammonia & \textbf{Experimental} --- set by media formulation or measured at inlet \\

$C_{L,\text{out}}^\text{lac}$ & Effluent concentration of lactic acid & \textbf{Experimental} --- measured at outlet (e.g.\ enzymatic assay, HPLC) \\
\hline
$C_{L,\text{out}}^\text{NH}$ & Effluent concentration of ammonia & \textbf{Experimental} --- measured at outlet (e.g.\ colorimetric assay) \\
\hline
$X$ & Biomass density within the hydrogel & \textbf{Experimental} --- requires destructive assay (DNA quantification, protein content, or cell counting after digestion) \\
\hline
$\rho$ & Fluid density & \textbf{Literature} --- well characterised for aqueous culture media at physiological conditions \\
\hline
$v$ & Fluid velocity inside the hydrogel & \textbf{Derived} --- calculated from $Q_\text{in}$ and the cross-sectional flow area (geometry measured experimentally) \\
\hline
$\mu$ & Dynamic viscosity of the fluid & \textbf{Literature} --- well characterised for aqueous culture media at physiological conditions \\
\hline
$Re$ & Reynolds number of fluid inside the hydrogel ($Re = \rho v L / \mu$, where $L$ is a characteristic length) & \textbf{Derived} --- calculated from $\rho$, $v$, $\mu$, and geometry; $\rho$ and $\mu$ from literature, $v$ and geometry from experiment \\
