Parameter Definition Literature or Experimental?
Q_in Volumetric flow rate at the inlet Experimental — set by the pump/flow controller; a design/operational variable
Q_out Volumetric flow rate at the outlet Experimental — measured directly; should match Q_in at steady state
BLT Boundary layer thickness (wall thickness of the hydrogel "teabag") Experimental — measured physically (e.g. cryosectioning, microscopy, or calipers)
DL_lac Liquid phase diffusivity of lactic acid Literature — well characterised in aqueous media; values available at physiological conditions
DL_NH Liquid phase diffusivity of ammonia Literature — well characterised in aqueous media; values available at physiological conditions
CL_in_Lac Influent concentration of lactic acid Experimental — set by media formulation or measured at inlet
CL_in_NH Influent concentration of ammonia Experimental — set by media formulation or measured at inlet
CL_out_Lac Effluent concentration of lactic acid Experimental — measured at outlet (e.g. enzymatic assay, HPLC)
CL_out_NH Effluent concentration of ammonia Experimental — measured at outlet (e.g. colorimetric assay)
X Biomass density within the hydrogel Experimental — requires destructive assay (DNA quantification, protein content, or cell counting after digestion)
ρ Fluid density Literature — well characterised for aqueous culture media at physiological conditions
v Fluid velocity inside the hydrogel Derived — calculated from Q_in and the cross-sectional flow area (geometry measured experimentally)
μ Dynamic viscosity of the fluid Literature — well characterised for aqueous culture media at physiological conditions
RE Reynolds number of fluid inside the hydrogel (RE = ρvL/μ, where L is a characteristic length) Derived — calculated from ρ, v, μ, and geometry; ρ and μ from literature, v and geometry from experiment
